# AvidZenith LLC

**Technical work, taken to its proper depth.**

AvidZenith LLC is a Washington-based software and technology company focused on software platforms, embedded systems, decision-support tools, and research-backed systems work.

We approach engineering as systems development rather than a collection of isolated features. Software, data, interfaces, hardware constraints, operational workflows, and long-term maintenance are considered together so the systems we build remain understandable, reliable, and adaptable as they grow.

## Areas of Work

* **Software & Platforms** — Applications, services, APIs, and platform systems designed for clarity, reliability, and long-term use.
* **Embedded & Hardware Systems** — Software and interfaces developed around real hardware, device, and operational constraints.
* **Decision-Support Tools** — Research-backed systems that turn technical, operational, and economic information into useful workflows.
* **Research & Development** — Internal exploration of software, intelligent systems, simulation, infrastructure, and interactive technologies.

## Selected Work

### Feed4Profit — University Collaboration

AvidZenith contributes to **Feed4Profit** in collaboration with the [Swine Applied Innovations Lab](https://faculty.sites.iastate.edu/dsrosero/) at Iowa State University.

Feed4Profit is a web-based decision-support platform for swine production that connects nutrition, predicted growth performance, market assumptions, and profitability-focused scenario comparison within a unified workflow.

AvidZenith's work includes software architecture, platform development and modernization, technical implementation, and the continued development of the system's underlying application foundation.

&rarr; [Visit Feed4Profit](https://feed4profit.net/)
<br>&rarr; [Read about Feed4Profit at Iowa State University](https://www.ipic.iastate.edu/news/feed4profit-compare-feeding-programs)

### SOLSTICE Panel — Open Source

**SOLSTICE Panel** is an open-source Rust-based management system for NVIDIA Jetson-powered embedded hardware.

It combines hardware control, device status, runtime configuration, process visibility, OLED and LED management, and a local browser-based interface into a single inspectable system.

SOLSTICE Panel is released under the MIT License and is open to community use, modification, and contribution.

&rarr; [AvidZenith/SOLSTICE-Panel](https://github.com/AvidZenith/SOLSTICE-Panel)

## How We Build

**Direct technical ownership**
<br>The people shaping our systems remain close to their design, implementation, and long-term behavior.

**Systems, not fragments**
<br>Architecture, data, interfaces, hardware constraints, workflows, and operations are treated as parts of the same design problem.

**Appropriate depth**
<br>We go deeply where correctness requires it and preserve simplicity where additional complexity provides no meaningful value.

**Built for continuity**
<br>Technical decisions should remain understandable, maintainable, and adaptable beyond the initial implementation.

**Details matter**
<br>Small technical decisions often determine how well a system behaves years later. We take the time to understand those details and make them matter.
